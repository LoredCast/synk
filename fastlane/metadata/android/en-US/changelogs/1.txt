Initial release (v1.0.0):
- C11 JNI + Java 3-way sync engine with LCS diff3 auto-merge & recursive inotify watcher
- WebDAV (Nextcloud/Seafile), FTP/FTPS, S3 SigV4, Local, CalDAV & CardDAV support
- Built-in local & remote file explorers
- Interactive Conflict Manager with editable 3-way merge previews
- Live sync progress telemetry (active file, speed, ETA, cancel)
- Fine-grained AMOLED settings page & JobScheduler periodic background syncs
